Clinical experience

Clinical training in psychiatric and outpatient settings

Alongside my research, I trained in a hospital psychiatry department and worked in an outpatient diagnostic centre in Dhaka. In both settings I took histories, used assessment tools, and counseled clients, and I saw how much an assessment depends on the person's language, family, and circumstances.

What each setting taught me

How it shapes my research

Assessment depends on context
Taking histories showed me how much a person's account of their distress is shaped by language, family, and circumstances.
Results must be usable
Psychoeducation taught me that assessment results only help when the people receiving them can understand and act on them.
From clinic to school
These lessons drive my interest in culturally validated tools and in assessment that leads to real support for students.
